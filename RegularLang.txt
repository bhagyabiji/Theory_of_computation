REGULAR LANGUAGES
-----------------
- language of DFA is Regular language
    Grammar
    ------
    G = (V, T, P, S)
    where,
      V - Non-terminals like A, B,C...
      T - terminals like a, c,d,...
      P - production rules 
      S- start symbol

      for eg:-
         S -> aSbS
         S -> bSaS
         S -> E(epsilon)
